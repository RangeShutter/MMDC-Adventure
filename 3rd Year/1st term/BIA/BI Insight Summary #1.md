* **Executive Summary**  
  FinMark Corporation’s selected KPIs focus on customer retention, client revenue, customer growth, customer engagement, and customer satisfaction, providing a balanced view of customer and business performance. The available datasets provide relevant variables for measuring these KPIs, including customer IDs, signup dates, engagement dates, billed amounts, engagement tiers, and customer feedback ratings. However, data-quality issues such as missing values, duplicate feedback records, inconsistent formatting, and unmatched service IDs should be considered when interpreting the results. Overall, the analysis can help FinMark identify customer behavior and performance patterns and support decisions related to customer retention, engagement, service value, and client growth.

* **Reflections & Recommendations**

  The analysis of FinMark Corporation’s five selected KPIs provides a balanced view of customer behavior, financial performance, client growth, engagement, and satisfaction. The findings show that these KPIs should be analyzed together because changes in one area may affect another. For example, an increase in the number of clients may not necessarily indicate improved business performance if customer engagement, retention, or revenue per client remains low. Therefore, FinMark should use the KPIs as a combined performance framework rather than evaluating each metric independently.

* **KPIs to Monitor More Closely:** FinMark should closely monitor **Customer Retention Rate (CRR)** and **Customer Engagement Rate (CER)** because they can provide early indicators of customer inactivity or potential customer loss. **Average Revenue per Client (ARPC)** should also be monitored to determine whether client growth is generating sufficient financial value. **Client Growth Rate (CGR)** can be used to evaluate progress toward the company’s goal of increasing its SME client base, while **Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)** can help ensure that growth and revenue improvements do not negatively affect customer experience.  
* **Areas Where Additional Data Would Be Helpful:** Additional data could improve the reliability and depth of the analysis. FinMark could collect more detailed customer information, such as customer segment, industry, location, acquisition source, and customer tenure. More complete engagement data, including engagement type, service used, transaction frequency, and service value, would also help explain customer behavior. For CSAT, additional feedback details such as feedback category, service involved, and reason for the rating could help identify specific causes of customer dissatisfaction.  
* **Suggested Next Steps for Dashboard or Story Development:** The Tableau dashboard should present the five KPIs using clear KPI cards, trend charts, and comparison visuals. Filters for **date, customer segment, service, and engagement tier** can allow users to investigate changes in performance. The dashboard could also include a combined view showing the relationship between client growth, retention, engagement, revenue, and satisfaction. Data-quality indicators should be included where appropriate to highlight missing, duplicate, or inconsistent records. Overall, the dashboard should focus on actionable insights that help FinMark identify at-risk customers, evaluate growth opportunities, improve engagement, and maintain customer satisfaction while increasing revenue.
