1. **Final KPI Selection Table**

| KPI Name | Definition / What It Measures | Why This KPI Matters to FinMark | Supporting Dataset, Variables & Calculation |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Customer Retention Rate (CRR)**  | Measures the percentage of existing customers who remain active with FinMark during a defined period.  | Helps FinMark determine how effectively it retains existing clients. A high retention rate can indicate strong customer relationships, while a declining rate can signal potential customer loss and the need for retention strategies.  | finmark\_clients.csv \+ finmark\_engagements.csv — customer\_id, signup\_date, engagement\_date, engagement\_tier. Formula: ((Customers at end of period − New customers acquired during the period) ÷ Customers at beginning of period) × 100\.  |
| **Average Revenue per Client (ARPC)**  | Measures the average revenue generated by each active client during a defined period.  | Helps FinMark measure the financial value of its client base and determine whether client growth is translating into revenue growth.  | finmark\_engagements.csv \+ finmark\_clients.csv — customer\_id, billed\_amount, engagement\_date, engagement\_tier. Formula: Total billed\_amount during the period ÷ Number of unique active clients during the period.  |
| **Client Growth Rate(CGR)** | Measures the percentage increase or decrease in FinMark's client base over a defined period. | Directly supports **FinMark's strategic goal of growing its SME client base by 40%**. It allows management to monitor whether customer acquisition is progressing toward the target and identify periods of stronger or weaker growth. | finmark\_clients.csv — customer\_id, signup\_date. Formula: ((Clients at end of period − Clients at beginning of period) ÷ Clients at beginning of period) × 100\. Client counts are based on unique customer\_id values. |
| **Customer Engagement Rate (CER)** | *Measures the percentage of FinMark clients who have at least one recorded engagement during a defined period.* | ***Helps FinMark identify how actively its clients are interacting with its services**. A declining engagement rate can signal inactive customers and provide an opportunity for targeted retention or re-engagement strategies.* | *finmark\_clients.csv \+ finmark\_engagements.csv — customer\_id, engagement\_id, engagement\_date. Formula: (Unique clients with ≥1 engagement during the period ÷ Total active clients during the period) × 100\.* |
| **Customer satisfaction Score (CSAT)** | *Measures the average rating given by customers in their feedback submissions.* | *Helps FinMark monitor customer satisfaction and service quality. It can identify areas that need improvement and support customer retention strategies. It was selected because it directly measures customer experience, complementing client Growth Rate and Customer Engagement Rate.* | *Finmark\_client\_feedback.csv – Rating, feedback\_id, customer\_id, submitted\_at. Formula: Sum of customer ratings ÷ number of valid ratings.* |
