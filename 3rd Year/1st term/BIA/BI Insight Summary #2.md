1. **Executive Summary**  
   *This BI Insight Summary #2 focuses on data issues that may affect the accuracy of FinMark’s KPI analysis. The major issues investigated are unmatched service IDs, inconsistent engagement quantity formats, and missing or duplicate customer feedback. Unmatched service IDs limit service-level revenue analysis and can understate ARPC when an inner join removes unmatched engagements. Quantity-format inconsistencies affect service-usage analysis but do not directly change CER as currently defined. Missing and duplicate ratings affect CSAT. The findings show that FinMark should improve data quality before using these results for major decisions.*  
     
2. **Root Cause Analysis & Recommendations**  
   *\<Document the analysis result in the subsections below. You may include additional sections based on the number of identified anomalies.\>*  
     
   1. **Issue \#1:** ***Unmatched Service IDs Affecting Revenue Analysis***  
      * **Observed Anomaly or Underperformance:** *Average Revenue per Client (ARPC) measures the average revenue generated by each active client. It helps FinMark determine whether client growth is also creating financial value. However, the engagement dataset contains service IDs from PROD001 to PROD020, while the service dataset only contains PROD001 to PROD005. Because of this, 754 engagement records cannot currently be matched to a service name, category, or price.*  
      * **Root Cause Investigation:** *The main cause is an incomplete service reference dataset. Missing reference records for PROD006-PROD020 make it impossible to assign service names, categories, and prices to those engagements. ARPC calculated directly from `billed_amount` and `customer_id` remains available, but an inner join to the incomplete service table would exclude unmatched engagements and understate revenue and ARPC.*  
      * **Supporting Visuals:**

**Service Reference Coverage and Revenue at Risk**

| Service Match Status | Engagement Records | Record Share | Billed Amount | Revenue Share |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Matched (PROD001-PROD005) | 266 | 26.1% | $13,212,967.16 | 25.9% |
| Unmatched (PROD006-PROD020) | 754 | 73.9% | $37,760,810.36 | 74.1% |
| **Total** | **1,020** | **100.0%** | **$50,973,777.52** | **100.0%** |

*Tableau chart specification: Create a 100% stacked horizontal bar using Service Match Status as color and Engagement Records as the measure. Label each segment with its record count and percentage; include Billed Amount and Revenue Share in the tooltip.*

*Source: `FinMark_Engagements.csv` joined to `finmark_services.csv` by `service_id`. Matched means the service ID exists in the service reference table.*

      * **Business impact:** *This issue can affect management and the sales team because 73.9% of engagements and 74.1% of billed revenue cannot be attributed to a named service or category. An inner join would omit $37,760,810.36 from the analysis, which could mislead service-performance, resource-allocation, pricing, and ARPC decisions.*  
      * **Data-Driven Recommendation: Based on the findings,** *FinMark should complete and validate the service master list for PROD006-PROD020 before making major revenue decisions. After correcting the records, the company should recalculate service-level revenue and compare ARPC calculated from the engagement data with ARPC after the completed service join to confirm that no engagement revenue is excluded.*  
          
   2. **Issue \#2:** ***Inconsistent Engagement Quantity Records Affecting Engagement Analysis***   
      * **Observed Anomaly or Underperformance:** *Customer Engagement Rate (CER) measures the percentage of FinMark clients who have at least one recorded engagement during a defined period. The quantity field is not required for this definition of CER. However, the engagement dataset contains **inconsistent quantity formats**, which can affect engagement-volume, transaction, and service-usage analysis.*   
      * **Root Cause Investigation:** *The main cause is inconsistent data entry in the engagement quantity field. Of 1,020 records, 788 use numeric values, while 232 store the quantity as the text `2 units`. All observed values can be standardized, but leaving the field in mixed formats may prevent correct numeric aggregation in Tableau.*   
      * **Supporting Visuals:**

**Raw Engagement Quantity Format**

| Raw Quantity Value | Records | Record Share | Format Status | Standardized Quantity |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- | ---: |
| `1` | 274 | 26.9% | Numeric | 1 |
| `2` | 239 | 23.4% | Numeric | 2 |
| `2 units` | 232 | 22.7% | Non-standard text | 2 |
| `3` | 275 | 27.0% | Numeric | 3 |
| **Total** | **1,020** | **100.0%** | **788 numeric / 232 non-standard** | — |

*Tableau chart specification: Create a bar chart with Raw Quantity Value on Columns and Number of Records on Rows. Color the bars by Format Status, display count and percentage labels, and annotate `2 units` as the value requiring normalization.*

*Source: `FinMark_Engagements.csv` (`quantity`). Standardization rule: convert `2 units` to numeric value `2`.*

      * **Business impact:**  
        *The 232 non-standard records represent 22.7% of all engagements. If Tableau treats quantity as text or returns null during conversion, FinMark may undercount service units and misidentify high- or low-usage clients. The current CER is not affected because it counts clients with at least one engagement rather than summing quantity.*   
      * **Data-Driven Recommendation:** *FinMark should convert `2 units` to numeric `2` and establish numeric validation rules for future quantity records. After standardization, the company should recalculate engagement-volume and service-usage metrics. CER should continue to use unique engaged clients, while quantity should be used as a separate measure of usage intensity.*   
          
   3. **Issue \#3: Missing and Duplicate Feedback Records Affecting CSAT**   
      * **Observed Anomaly or Underperformance:** *Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT) measures the average rating provided by customers through feedback submissions. It helps FinMark monitor customer satisfaction and service quality. The feedback dataset contains **missing ratings and duplicate feedback records**, which may affect the accuracy of the overall satisfaction score.*   
      * **Root Cause Investigation:** *The main causes are incomplete customer feedback submissions and repeated `feedback_id` values. Missing ratings cannot be used to calculate a satisfaction score, while duplicate records give repeated responses more weight. The raw file contains 60 rows but only 50 unique feedback IDs. After retaining one record per feedback ID, 12 records still have missing ratings and 38 valid ratings remain for CSAT.*   
      * **Supporting Visuals:**

**Effect of Feedback Cleaning on CSAT**

| Data-Quality Measure | Raw Data | Cleaned Data |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Feedback rows | 60 | 50 |
| Duplicate rows | 10 | 0 |
| Missing ratings | 14 | 12 |
| Valid ratings used for CSAT | 46 | 38 |
| Average CSAT | 3.54 / 5.00 | 3.45 / 5.00 |

*Tableau chart specification: Build side-by-side bars for Raw Data and Cleaned Data using Valid Ratings and Missing Ratings, then add KPI labels for average CSAT (3.54 versus 3.45). Show the 10 removed duplicate rows as an annotation.*

*Source: `FinMark_client_feedback.csv`. Cleaning sequence: retain one record per `feedback_id`, then exclude blank ratings from the CSAT average.*

      * **Business impact:**  
        *Missing and duplicate feedback can cause the CSAT result to be misleading. Cleaning reduces the valid rating count from 46 to 38 and changes CSAT from 3.54 to 3.45. Using the uncleaned result could cause FinMark to overstate satisfaction and misdirect customer-experience improvements.*   
      * **Data-Driven Recommendation:** *FinMark should remove or resolve confirmed duplicate feedback IDs and validate missing ratings where possible. The company should also implement uniqueness and required-rating rules when collecting feedback. Once the data is cleaned, FinMark should use the 38 valid unique ratings to report the current CSAT of 3.45 and regularly monitor satisfaction trends.*
